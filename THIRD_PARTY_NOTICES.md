# Third-Party Notices

LANtern uses or interoperates with the following third-party projects. Their
respective licenses continue to apply to those components.

## Microsoft Windows Driver Samples

The LANtern Indirect Display Driver is derived from Microsoft's Indirect
Display Driver sample. The original sample and its license are available at:

https://github.com/microsoft/Windows-driver-samples

## FFmpeg

LANtern invokes FFmpeg for display capture and H.264 encoding. FFmpeg builds
may be licensed under LGPL or GPL depending on their configuration. Binary
distributions of LANtern must include the notices, corresponding license
information, and source-code offer required by the selected FFmpeg build.

https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

## MediaMTX

LANtern uses MediaMTX as its local RTSP and WebRTC media gateway.

https://github.com/bluenviron/mediamtx

## QRCoder

LANtern uses QRCoder to generate viewer QR codes.

https://github.com/codebude/QRCoder

## Inno Setup

Windows installation packages are built using Inno Setup. Inno Setup is a
build-time dependency and is not part of the LANtern source code.

https://jrsoftware.org/isinfo.php
